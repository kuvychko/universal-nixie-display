# Nixie HV module: bench data (Amazon B09D94N3MX)

Reported by the user 2026-11-01. 12 V input from a bench supply. Module adjusted with its trimmer.
Output power and efficiency are calculated from the measured voltage, load and input current.

## Inrush at 12 V switch-on (2026-11-06)

Reported by the user. 1 Ω series shunt in the 12 V feed, module under load, Siglent SDS804X HD:
C1 DC 1 MΩ, 2 V/div, 20 MHz BW limit, 1 ms/div, single edge trigger at 200 mV rising (1 V = 1 A).

Photos of the scope screen, trimmed and flattened with `tools/rectify_scope_photo.py`.

| Capture | Peak | Time back to the plateau | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7.3 A | ≈ 1.2 ms | Y cursors, 7.30 V ([photo](2026-11-06_inrush_capture1.jpg)) |
| 2 | ≈ 6.8 A | 1.19 ms | X cursors; peak read off the graticule ([photo](2026-11-06_inrush_capture2.jpg)) |

Longer capture 3 ([photo](2026-11-06_inrush_capture3.jpg)): 500 mV/div, 50 ms/div, same
trigger. After the spike the input sits on a plateau of ≈ 0.6 A (read off the graticule) for
≈ 35-40 ms, then steps down to the steady **0.358 A** (Y cursors, 358 mV). Start-up is complete
within ≈ 40 ms of switch-on.

Operating point for these captures (user): bench supply 12 V at 0.329 A on its own meter, module
output 173.0 V into the same 11.002 kΩ load. Calculated: 15.7 mA, 2.72 W out, 3.95 W from the
supply, 68.9 % including the ≈ 0.11 W lost in the shunt (≈ 71 % at the module's own input,
≈ 11.67 V). This matches the 175 V full-load row above.

**Reading accuracy:** the Y cursor on capture 3 gave 0.358 A against the supply meter's
0.329 A. The difference is 29 mV, about 0.06 div at 500 mV/div, and is within how precisely a
cursor can be set on a trace thickened by the converter's switching ripple (user's assessment).
Treat the cursor values as approximate, ±0.1 div or so. A gated Mean measurement would average
the ripple out.

The pulse is a single exponential decay, consistent with charging the module's input capacitance:
**470 µF 25 V** electrolytic at the module's 12 V input (marking read by the user, 2026-11-06).
The 1 Ω shunt is part of the loop and limits the peak, so the inrush without it is higher than
measured here. Power was applied hot (user): bench supply already on at 12 V, alligator clip
connected to the module input, so the edge is not slowed by the supply's own output ramp. Clip
contact bounce is the likely source of the small spikes just before the main pulse.

## Not measured yet

- Inrush without the 1 Ω shunt (smaller shunt or current probe)
- Trimmer end points (at least 150-204 V). Upper end deliberately not measured: the module may
  have no internal overvoltage protection (user, 2026-11-06). The HV5622 tube drivers need ≤ 220 V;
  see the HV limit in `docs/hardware.md`
- Load regulation at one fixed trimmer setting
- Output ripple, overshoot at start-up, temperature after a long run, audible noise
- Decay under the full load
- Decimal-point maintaining voltage on a real tube (sets the DP series resistors)
