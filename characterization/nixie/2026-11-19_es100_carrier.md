# ES100 carrier (Universal Solder): pulls on the header lines

Reported by the user 2026-11-19. Carrier unpowered, meter in resistance mode.

| Line | Found on the carrier |
|---|---|
| SDA | 10 kΩ pull-up |
| SCL | 10 kΩ pull-up |
| IRQ- | 5 MΩ: effectively no pull |
| EN | 15 MΩ: effectively no pull |

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-19)

* **R305 / R306 (Nixie board's WWVB_SDA / WWVB_SCL pull-ups): not fitted.** The ES100 is alone
  on the Nixie RP2040's I2C0, so the carrier's 10k pair is the bus's only pull-up. The bus runs
  at 100 kHz: 0.8473 × R × C ≤ 1000 ns (UM10204, standard mode) allows ≈ 118 pF with 10 kΩ,
  well above a short harness, the ES100 and one RP2040 pin. At 400 kHz (300 ns) it would allow
  only ≈ 35 pF, which would need R305 / R306 in parallel.
* **R307 (IRQ- pull-up) and R308 (EN pull-down): 10k** from the kit. With nothing on the carrier,
  R308 alone holds the ES100 in shutdown while the RP2040 is in reset or being flashed.
