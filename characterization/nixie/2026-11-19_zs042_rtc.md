# ZS-042 RTC module (DS3231): pins, pull-ups, charging path

Reported by the user 2026-11-19. Module unpowered, meter in resistance mode.

## Header

4-pin header, in order: SCL, SDA, VCC, GND. The Nixie board's J302 is +3V3, GND, SDA, SCL, so the
harness is wired by silk name, not position (assembly Stage 6).

## I2C pull-ups

| Measurement | Reading |
|---|---|
| GND to SCL | 45 kΩ |
| GND to SDA | 45 kΩ |
| SDA, SCL to VCC | 4.7 kΩ pull-ups present |

The readings to GND go through the module's VCC-to-GND path, so they don't show the pull-ups on
their own. The pull-ups are on two 3-resistor SMD arrays on the module.

Decision (user): **pull-ups kept**, counted in parallel with the dekatron board's 4.7 kΩ pair:
≈ 2.35 kΩ to 3.3 V, ≈ 1.4 mA when a line is pulled low. That is within the 3 mA at 0.4 V every
I2C device must sink (NXP UM10204, standard and fast mode). The DS3231's own rating isn't checked
yet: its datasheet isn't in `docs/datasheets/`. Both pull-up rails come from the same 5 V
(NIX_LV), so they power up and down together.

## Coin-cell charging path

A diode and a resistor in series from VCC to the cell are present (values not read).

Decision (user): **LIR2032** (rechargeable) fitted, path kept. Still to check against the DS3231
datasheet and the cell's: what the path charges the cell to from a 3.3 V VCC (one diode drop below
VCC, below a lithium-ion cell's full charge), and whether that keeps VBAT in the DS3231's range.
